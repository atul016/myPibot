---
name: face_me
description: Turn your whole body to face whoever is talking to you -- you heard which way their voice came from.
where: voice
needs: body
---
When they ask you to turn toward them or to face them: "turn towards me", "face me", "turn around, I'm over here".
Not a turn they name ("turn left" -- that's move), not coming to them ("come here" -- that's drive_to).
