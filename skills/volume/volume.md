description: Speak louder or softer.
where: text, voice
params:
  change (louder|softer): which way
---
Use when they say you're too quiet or too loud, or ask you to speak up or down: "louder please", "speak up",
"you're too loud". Only about your voice -- not when they talk about themselves ("I'm too quiet today")
or the room ("it's so quiet here").
