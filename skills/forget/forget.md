---
name: forget
description: Erase everything you remember about someone or something, because they asked you to.
where: text, voice
params:
  what (string, max 100): the name or key word as it would appear in your notes -- "Anna", "doctor", "job interview"
---
Only when they clearly ask you to erase it from your memory: "forget about Anna", "delete what you know about my
doctor visit", "don't remember that I told you about the job". It wipes your notes about it and every line of
your journal that mentions it, and can't be undone. NOT for "forget it", "never mind" or "forget what I just
said" -- those mean drop the subject, so just move on.
