---
name: move
description: Move a little -- forward, back, turn, dance, or a playful fist bump or charge.
where: voice
needs: body
params:
  how (forward|backward|turn left|turn right|dance|fist bump|bullfight): which move
---
Use when they ask for one of these small moves: "go back a bit", "come forward", "turn left", "dance for me",
"fist bump!", or say yes when you just offered one. Not for coming to them ("come here", "come closer", "come towards me":
that's drive_to), driving to something (drive_to), following (follow_me) or turning to face them ("turn towards
me", "face me": that's face_me).
Not for talk that only mentions a word: "welcome back", "my back hurts", "go ahead and tell me", "do you like to dance?".
